How to play (30 seconds)
  • Your turn: drive once — Careful (2 steps) or Fast (4 steps, but animals next to where you stop flee) — and take one photo (before, during, or after driving).
  • Drive one tile at a time, flipping face-down land around your jeep. Driving through an animal's tile scatters it.
  • A photo captures the 3×3 window around your jeep: each animal scores its value, plus herds (3+ of a kind +4), action shots (predator + prey +5), water scenes (2+ animals with water +2), and Golden Hour (+4 at Dawn/Dusk).
  • You have 8 exposures for the whole game (10 rounds over 2 days). The Field Guide shows every animal in the wild — the full manifest is public.
  • Nature moves every round: the event card steers the herds, predators stalk and eat prey, animals you photographed scatter (the moment passes), and animals can flee off the board for good.
  • Complete your two secret assignments and the Big Five (🦁🐆🐘🦏🐃) for big bonuses.
