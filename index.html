How to play (30 seconds)
  • Your turn: drive once (Careful = 2 tiles, Fast = 4 tiles but nearby animals flee), and optionally take one photo.
  • Driving reveals face-down land around your jeep. Animals hide under tiles.
  • A photo captures the 3×3 window around your jeep. You score every animal in frame, plus bonuses: herds (3+ of a kind +4), action shots (predator + prey +5), waterhole scenes (+2), and Golden Hour (+4 at Dawn/Dusk).
  • You have 8 exposures for the whole game (10 rounds over 2 days).
  • Complete your two secret assignments and the Big Five (🦁🐘🦏🐆🐃) for big bonuses.
  • At day's end, nature moves: rain pulls herds to water, predators hunt, migrations arrive.
