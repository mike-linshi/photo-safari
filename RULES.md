# GOLDEN HOUR — Tabletop Rules (Print & Play)

*A photo safari game for 2–4 players · ~45–60 minutes · ages 10+*

> You are wildlife photographers with one roll of film each. Drive the savanna,
> discover what hides beneath the grass, and choose your moments — the light won't wait.

---

## Components

| Qty | Component |
|---|---|
| 81 | Terrain tiles (9×9 board): grassland, acacia bush, marsh, waterhole, kopje (rock), 4 camp tiles |
| ~36 | Animal standees (12 species, see roster below) |
| 4 | Safari jeeps (player tokens) |
| 4 | **Viewfinder frames** — a 3×3 open window (the game's signature piece; cut from card or clear acetate) |
| 32 | Film tokens (8 per player) |
| 12 | Event cards (Nature deck) |
| 12 | Secret Assignment cards |
| 1 | Day track board (Dawn → Morning → Midday → Afternoon → Dusk, ×2 days) + sun marker |
| 4 | Score pads / photo log sheets |

**Tile anatomy:** each tile shows terrain on its face. Roughly half the tiles also
show a small animal icon — when the tile is flipped face-up, place that animal's
standee on it. This is how the savanna "populates itself" with zero bookkeeping.

## Animal roster

| Animal | Value | Notes | Animal | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Impala | 1 | prey | Hippo | 4 | water only |
| Wildebeest | 1 | prey | Crocodile | 4 | predator, water |
| Zebra | 2 | prey | Elephant | 5 | **Big Five** |
| Flamingo | 2 | prey, water | Lion | 5 | **Big Five**, predator |
| Giraffe | 3 | | Leopard | 7 | **Big Five**, predator |
| Buffalo | 3 | **Big Five** | Rhino | 8 | **Big Five** |

Rarity is built into the tile mix: impala appear on ~5 tiles, rhino and leopard on only 2 each.

## Setup

1. Place the 4 **camp tiles** in the corners, face-up.
2. Shuffle all remaining tiles and lay them **face-down** in the 9×9 grid.
3. Flip face-up every tile adjacent (including diagonals) to a camp. (Animal-icon
   tiles never sit next to camps — the mix guarantees a quiet start.)
4. Each player: a jeep on their camp, 8 film tokens, a viewfinder frame, and
   **2 secret Assignment cards** (dealt from the shuffled deck; keep hidden).
5. Shuffle the Nature deck. Put the sun marker on **Day 1 – Dawn**.

## The Day Track

The game lasts **10 rounds**: Dawn, Morning, Midday, Afternoon, Dusk — twice.

- **Dawn and Dusk are Golden Hour:** every photo taken then scores **+4**.
- The sun advances at the end of each round. After the second Dusk, the game ends.

## On Your Turn

Do these in any order (each at most once):

### 1. Drive (choose one speed)

- **🐢 Careful Drive** — move up to **2 tiles** orthogonally.
- **💨 Fast Drive** — move up to **4 tiles**, but you **spook**: after stopping,
  every animal adjacent to your jeep (including diagonals) flees 1 tile directly
  away from you (if blocked on all escape routes, it stands its ground).

**Discovery:** whenever your jeep enters or passes a tile, flip **all face-down
tiles adjacent to it** (including diagonals). If a flipped tile shows an animal
icon, place that standee on it immediately.

**Terrain:** entering marsh costs 2 movement. Water and kopje tiles block jeeps
(animals may occupy them; only water animals may occupy water). You may never
enter a tile holding an animal or another jeep. If a flip reveals blocking
terrain in your path, you stop short — that's safari driving.

### 2. Take a Photo (optional, costs 1 film token)

Center your **viewfinder frame** on your jeep. Everything inside the 3×3 window
is your photograph. Score it immediately on your photo log:

| In frame | Points |
|---|---|
| Each animal | its value |
| 2 of the same species | +1 (pair) |
| 3+ of the same species | +4 (herd!) |
| Predator **and** prey together | +5 (action shot) |
| 2+ animals **and** a water tile | +2 (waterhole scene) |
| Taken at Dawn or Dusk | +4 (Golden Hour) |
| A predator marked as *hunting* | +5 (see events) |

Also check off any Big Five species in the frame, and reveal + score any
**secret assignment** the photo completes.

*A photo of empty grass scores 0. Nobody publishes that.*

## End of Round — Nature Moves

After all players have had a turn, flip the top **Nature card** and resolve it:

- **🌧 Rain Sweeps In** — every animal moves 1 tile toward the nearest revealed water.
- **🐂 The Migration** — place 6 herd animals (4 wildebeest, 2 zebra) on one board
  edge (roll for which); flip those tiles.
- **🦁 The Hunt** — each predator moves up to 2 tiles toward the nearest prey animal.
  Any predator that ends adjacent to prey gets a *hunting* marker for the next
  round (photos of it: +5).
- **✨ Perfect Light** — all photos next round +3.
- **🌪 Dust Storm** — next round: photos −3 and no Fast Drive.
- **🦛 The Watering Call** — every animal within 3 of water moves 1 tile toward it.

(Animals moving always avoid jeeps, other animals, camps, and — unless they're
water animals — water tiles. When forced to choose, the moving player picks.)

Then advance the sun. After Day 2's Dusk, the safari ends.

## Final Scoring

- Sum of all your photo scores (already logged)
- **Big Five bonus:** photographed all of 🦁 lion, 🐘 elephant, 🦏 rhino, 🐆 leopard,
  🐃 buffalo (across any photos) — **+15**
- Secret assignments already scored when completed (+6 to +8 each)

Highest total wins. Tiebreaker: most Big Five species, then most distinct species.

## Strategy Primer

- Film is your real currency: 8 shots, 10 rounds. A 6-point photo now may cost
  you a 20-point herd-at-the-waterhole at Golden Hour later.
- Fast Drive covers ground and flips more tiles — but scatters the very animals
  you came for. Approach your subject slowly.
- Watch the Nature deck: rain and the watering call *cluster* animals. The best
  photographers position one round ahead of the herd.

## Variants

- **First Safari (family):** ignore secret assignments and the spook rule.
- **Restless Savanna (advanced):** after the Nature card, each player rolls a
  direction die for one animal of their choice — it wanders 1 tile.
- **The Editor's Cut (expert):** take up to 8 photos but only your best 5 count
  at game end. Brutal, wonderful.
