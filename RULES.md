# GOLDEN HOUR — Tabletop Rules (Print & Play) · v2

*A photo safari game for 2–4 players · ~45–60 minutes · ages 10+*

> You are wildlife photographers with one roll of film each. Drive the savanna
> one tile at a time, discover what hides beneath the grass, and choose your
> moments — the light won't wait, and neither will the animals.

---

### The tile mix (public knowledge — like Daybreak, the distribution is printed on the box)

| Tile | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Grassland | 42 | open driving |
| Acacia bush | 12 | open driving |
| Marsh | 8 | costs 2 movement to enter |
| Waterhole | 8 | jeeps can't enter; water animals can |
| Kopje (rock) | 7 | jeeps can't enter; cats lurk here |

**Tile anatomy:** 28 of the 77 shuffled tiles have an animal icon printed on
matching terrain (the leopard is on an acacia tile, the hippo on a waterhole…).
Flip the tile, place the standee. The savanna populates itself with zero
bookkeeping — and because the manifest is public, you always know what's still
out there.

## Setup

1. Place the 4 **camp tiles** in the corners, face-up.
2. Shuffle the other 77 tiles and lay them **face-down** in the 9×9 grid.
3. Flip face-up every tile adjacent (including diagonals) to a camp; place any
   revealed animals. (Yes, sometimes an elephant is grazing outside camp at
   dawn. Lucky you.)
4. Each player: a jeep on their camp, 8 film tokens, a viewfinder frame, and
   **2 secret Assignment cards** (keep hidden).
5. Shuffle the Nature deck. Sun marker on **Day 1 – Dawn**.

## The Day Track

**10 rounds**: Dawn, Morning, Midday, Afternoon, Dusk — twice.
**Dawn and Dusk are Golden Hour:** every photo taken then scores **+4**.
After the second Dusk, the game ends.

## On Your Turn

You may **drive once** and **take one photo**, in any order — you may even
photograph mid-drive, then keep driving.

### Driving — one tile at a time

Choose a speed, then move your jeep **one orthogonal tile at a time**:

- **🐢 Careful Drive** — up to **2 steps**.
- **💨 Fast Drive** — up to **4 steps**, but when you stop, every animal
  adjacent to your jeep (including diagonals) **flees** 1 tile directly away
  from you. A flight off the board edge means it's gone for good; an animal
  with nowhere to run stands its ground.

After **each step**, flip all face-down tiles adjacent to your jeep (including
diagonals) and place any revealed animals.

**Animals never block you.** You may drive *through* a tile holding an animal —
it immediately flees 1 tile away from you (if it can't flee anywhere, you can't
push through). Scattering your own photo subject is legal, occasionally wise,
and always a little heartbreaking.

**Terrain:** marsh costs 2 steps to enter. Waterholes and kopjes block jeeps.
You can't drive through another jeep.

### Taking a photo (costs 1 film token, once per turn)

Center your **viewfinder frame** on your jeep. Everything in the 3×3 window is
your photograph. Score it immediately:

| In frame | Points |
|---|---|
| Each animal | its value |
| 3+ of the same species | +4 (herd!) |
| Predator **and** prey together | +5 (action shot) |
| 2+ animals **and** a water tile | +2 (waterhole scene) |
| Taken at Dawn or Dusk | +4 (Golden Hour) |

Check off any Big Five species in the frame, reveal + score any completed
**secret assignment** — and then put a *restless* marker on **every animal in
the photo**. The moment passes (see below).

*A photo of empty grass scores 0. Nobody publishes that.*

## End of Round — The Nature Phase

The savanna moves **every round**, in three steps:

**1. The Nature card steers the herds.** Flip the top card:

| Card (×2 each) | Every **prey** animal… |
|---|---|
| 🌧 Rain Sweeps In | moves 1 toward the nearest revealed water |
| 🦛 The Watering Call | within 3 of water: moves 1 toward it |
| 🍃 Restless Herds | moves 1 in a random direction (roll a direction die) |
| 🚙 Skittish Plains | moves 1 directly away from the nearest jeep |
| 🐂 The Migration | 5 newcomers (3 impala, 2 zebra) enter from a random edge, then all prey move 1 randomly |
| 😴 Still Air | stays put — the only quiet card |

**2. Predators hunt — every round, always.** Each predator moves up to 2 tiles
toward the nearest prey within 4 tiles. If it ends adjacent to prey, **it eats
it**: remove the prey from the game, and lay the predator on its side — it is
**gorged 💤** and never moves or hunts again (but a sleeping lion is still a
lovely photograph, full value).

**3. The moment passes.** Every animal with a *restless* marker (photographed
this round) moves **2 random steps**, then loses the marker. You can never
take the same picture twice — herds you shot disperse, waterhole scenes
dissolve.

**Leaving the board:** whenever any movement (random, fleeing, scattering)
takes an animal past the board edge, it is gone from the game. The savanna
doesn't owe you a second chance.

*(Animal movement fine print: animals move only across face-up tiles, never
onto camps, jeeps, or other animals; only water animals may enter waterholes.
When a rule leaves a choice, the player who flipped the Nature card decides.)*

Then advance the sun. After Day 2's Dusk, the safari ends.

## Final Scoring

- Sum of all photo scores (logged as you played)
- **Big Five bonus:** photographed all of 🦁🐆🐘🦏🐃 across your photos — **+15**
- Secret assignments were scored when completed (+6 to +9 each)

Highest total wins. Tiebreaker: most Big Five species, then most distinct species.

## Strategy Primer

- Film is your real currency: 8 shots, 10 rounds — and now the scene you're
  saving film for may walk away, get eaten, or bolt off the board. Shoot the
  moment, not the plan.
- The manifest is public: two leopards exist, both love acacia and kopje. Go
  where your subject *must* be.
- Predators are a clock. That herd by the water is one Hunt step away from
  being an action shot — or from being lunch. Both are worth points; only one
  of them waits for you.
- Fast Drive flips more tiles but scatters what you find. Arrive slow.

## Variants

- **First Safari (family):** ignore secret assignments and the spook rule.
- **Quiet Plains (gentler):** predators hunt only every *other* round.
- **The Editor's Cut (expert):** take up to 8 photos but only your best 5
  count at game end. Brutal, wonderful.
